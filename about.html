Our Story

Founded with a vision to elevate local Indonesian commodities, NusaPraya Export is a trusted supplier of premium coconut derivatives and authentic spices. We work directly with local farmers and manufacturers across the archipelago to ensure the highest quality from source to shipment.

We believe that Indonesian agriculture holds the key to global sustainable supply chains. By combining traditional farming wisdom with strict modern quality control, we deliver products that meet international standards.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the leading and most trusted exporter of Indonesian coconut and spice products, recognized globally for exceptional quality and sustainable practices.

Our Mission

To empower local farmers through fair trade, deliver consistent and premium-grade commodities to our global clients, and maintain transparency in every step of the supply chain.
